Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 October 2026

This policy explains what happens to information when you use Cipher. Cipher is a personal project run by Henry James Andrew Milne in South Australia, with adult supervision (see our Terms). Contact: henryjmilne23@outlook.com.

1. What Cipher collects

  • Your first name (or whatever name you type). It stays in your own browser.
  • Your chat history. It is saved in your own browser's local storage on your device. We don't keep a copy on our servers.
  • Your messages and any text file you attach. These are sent to us for a moment and passed on to the AI provider so you can get an answer (see section 3).
  • Technical details such as your IP address and device type, which our hosting provider (Vercel) may log when you visit.

Cipher has no accounts or passwords, and we don't ask for your email, phone number or address. Please don't type private details into your messages.

2. How we use it

  • To show your name, remember your chats on your device, and generate replies.
  • To keep Cipher working and secure.

We don't sell your information and don't use it for advertising. We don't use your messages to train AI models. What DeepSeek does with them is covered by its own policies. Cipher doesn't make decisions about people. It only produces text replies you can choose to use or ignore.

3. Who else handles your information

  • AI provider: your messages go to DeepSeek, which provides the AI models Cipher uses. It processes them to write a reply and may keep them under its own policies.
  • Vercel: hosts Cipher and may log request details. The adult supervisor who helps manage the hosting account may be able to see these logs.
  • Google Fonts: the Sora font loads from Google, so Google receives your IP address when the page loads.
  • Voice input (optional): if you use the microphone button, your browser may send your speech to its own speech-recognition service (for example Google, for Chrome) to turn it into text.

4. Sending information overseas

Cipher's AI provider, DeepSeek, is based in China, and our hosting and font providers may process information in the United States and elsewhere. Messages you send to Cipher may be stored or handled outside Australia, including in China. Please don't send anything you wouldn't want processed overseas.

5. Cookies and local storage

Cipher doesn't set advertising or tracking cookies and doesn't use analytics. It uses your browser's local storage to keep your name, chat history and chosen model on your device. Clearing your browser data erases them. Anyone who uses the same browser on the same device can see your saved chats.

6. How long we keep things

We don't store your chats on our servers. Local copies stay on your device until you clear your browser data. Our providers keep information for the periods in their own policies. Our hosting logs are kept for the time Vercel sets.

7. Security

Connections to Cipher use HTTPS, and our API keys are kept on the server, not in the page. No online service is perfectly secure, so please keep sensitive information out of your chats.

8. Children and teenagers

Cipher is aimed at students. If you are under 18, please ask a parent or guardian before using it, and never share personal details like your full name, address, school or phone number. Cipher is for people aged 14 and over. DeepSeek's own terms say people under 18 should use its services only with a parent or guardian's consent.

9. Your choices and rights

  • You can change your name or clear your history at any time by clearing your browser data.
  • You can ask us about the information we hold or ask us to fix it, at henryjmilne23@outlook.com. Because we don't keep your chats, there may be little to give you.
  • To delete information held by a provider, contact that provider.
  • If you have a privacy complaint, contact us first. You can also contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner at oaic.gov.au.

10. Changes

We may update this policy, and the "Last updated" date will change. Please check it from time to time.
