Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use Cipher.

Cipher is an independently developed AI-powered educational project operated in South Australia, Australia, with adult supervision and support.

Contact: henryjmilne23@outlook.com.

1. Accounts and personal information

Cipher does not require users to register, create an account, provide an email address or create a password.

When you open Cipher, you may enter a name to personalise your experience.

Your entered name is stored locally in your browser and is not intended to be stored in a central Cipher account database.

Please avoid entering your full legal name, home address, school name, phone number or other sensitive personal information.

2. Information stored on your device

Cipher uses browser local storage to save certain information on your device.

This may include:

  • Your chosen display name.
  • Your conversation history.
  • Saved memories that you have chosen to approve.
  • Your preferred AI model and other website settings.

This information is stored in your browser rather than intentionally saved in a central Cipher conversation database.

Anyone with access to your browser profile or device may be able to view locally stored information.

3. Messages and AI processing

When you send a message to Cipher, the message is transmitted through Cipher's hosting infrastructure to the AI provider so that a response can be generated.

Cipher currently uses DeepSeek for AI response generation.

Information sent to DeepSeek may include:

  • The message you submit.
  • Relevant conversation context required to generate a response.
  • Saved memories you have approved, where those memories are included in the AI request.
  • Other information necessary to process your request.

DeepSeek processes this information under its own terms and privacy policies. Its retention and data-handling practices may differ from Cipher's.

Cipher does not intentionally save conversation history in a central database. However, this does not mean that information transmitted to third-party providers is never retained.

4. How Cipher uses information

Information is used to:

  • Personalise your experience using your chosen display name.
  • Keep conversation history and approved memories available in your browser.
  • Generate AI responses to your messages.
  • Maintain, operate and protect Cipher.
  • Help prevent misuse and excessive requests.

Cipher does not sell users' personal information or use conversation contents for its own advertising.

Cipher does not independently use conversations to train its own AI models. Third-party AI providers may handle submitted information according to their own policies.

5. Third-party services

Cipher relies on third-party services that may process technical or user information.

DeepSeek

DeepSeek receives messages and relevant context submitted through Cipher to generate AI responses. Its own privacy and data retention policies apply to information it processes https://cdn.deepseek.com/policies/en-US/deepseek-privacy-policy.html.

Vercel

Vercel hosts the Cipher website and may process technical information such as IP addresses, request details and device information through its infrastructure and hosting logs.

Cipher may also use Vercel server-side functions to process AI requests and apply technical protections such as rate limiting.

Google Fonts

Cipher may load fonts from Google Fonts. When a browser requests these fonts, Google may receive technical information such as the user's IP address.

Browser speech recognition

If you use optional voice input, your browser or its speech-recognition provider may process audio to convert your speech into text. The handling of this information depends on the browser and provider being used.

6. Overseas information processing

Some third-party providers used by Cipher may process information outside Australia.

DeepSeek is based in China, and other providers, including Vercel and Google, may process information in the United States or other countries.

Information submitted through Cipher may therefore be processed or handled overseas, depending on the services involved.

Users should avoid submitting sensitive or confidential information.

7. Cookies and local storage

Cipher uses browser local storage to save preferences, names, conversation history and approved memories.

These items remain in the browser until they are deleted by the user or the browser's stored data is cleared.

Cipher does not intentionally use advertising or tracking cookies or analytics tools, subject to the actual configuration of the website and its hosting services.

Clearing browser storage may permanently remove saved conversations, names, memories and preferences.

8. Information retention

Cipher does not intentionally maintain a central database of users' conversation histories.

Locally stored information remains on the user's device until it is deleted or browser data is cleared.

Information sent to third-party services may be retained according to those providers' own policies and retention practices.

Technical hosting logs may be retained according to Vercel's applicable policies and settings.

9. Security

Cipher uses HTTPS to protect information transmitted between the browser and the website.

AI API keys are stored in server-side environment variables rather than intentionally exposed in the public website code.

Browser local storage is not encrypted by default. Users should keep their devices secure and avoid storing sensitive information in Cipher.

No internet service can guarantee complete security.

10. Children and teenagers

Cipher is designed for educational use and is intended for people aged 14 and over.

Users under 18 should obtain permission from a parent or guardian before using Cipher.

Users should avoid entering personal or sensitive information, including their full name, school, home address, phone number or private details about other people.

Third-party AI providers may have their own minimum age and parental consent requirements. Users should follow those requirements when using Cipher.

11. Your choices and privacy rights

You can manage information stored locally by:

  • Changing your display name through the website, if that feature is available.
  • Deleting saved memories through the Memory controls.
  • Clearing your conversation history using the available website controls.
  • Clearing Cipher's stored browser data through your browser settings.

Because Cipher does not maintain a central conversation database, it may not be able to recover conversations deleted from your device.

You can contact us about privacy questions, requests or concerns at henryjmilne23@outlook.com.

Requests concerning information held by third-party providers may need to be directed to those providers.

12. Privacy complaints

If you have a privacy concern or complaint, please contact us first at:

henryjmilne23@outlook.com

We will consider privacy complaints and respond where appropriate.

You may also contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (OAIC) through oaic.gov.au, subject to its jurisdiction and complaint requirements.

13. Changes to this Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as Cipher develops or its technology and third-party providers change.

The date at the top of this page indicates when it was last updated.

Users should periodically review this page for changes.
