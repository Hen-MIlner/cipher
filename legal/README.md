# Legal drafts

> [!WARNING]
> **Drafts only, not legal advice.** Have an Australian solicitor review them before anyone signs.

Plain-English draft agreements for **Cipher**, written for **South Australia**. They are adapted from standard structures, using the public-domain [General Legal templates](https://github.com/General-Legal/legal-templates) as a reference.

## What is here

| Document | Use it for |
|---|---|
| [Mutual NDA](mutual-nda.md) | Keeping information confidential when talking to a school, advisor, developer or partner |
| [Contributor and Advisor Agreement](contributor-advisor-agreement.md) | An unpaid contributor or advisor: confidentiality, who owns the work, student safety. No equity. |

## How to use them

1. Make a **copy** and fill in every `[bracket]`.
2. Work out who the legal party for Cipher is. A contract needs a company or an adult acting for the project. People under 18 can often cancel contracts, so a parent or guardian should sign too.
3. Get a solicitor to review the final version.
4. Keep the signed copy somewhere **private**.

## Rules for this folder

- Keep these files as **blank templates**.
- Never commit signed copies, names, addresses or any personal information. This repository is public.
- Anything involving student information needs separate data protection terms.
