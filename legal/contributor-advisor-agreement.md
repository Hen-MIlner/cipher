## Before you use this draft

- **What it does:** keeps Cipher's work confidential and makes sure the code, designs and ideas people create for Cipher belong to Cipher, so nobody is surprised later.
- **Who signs:** one agreement per person. Examples: an **advisor** who guides and oversees, and a **contributor** who helps with operations. The role description in clause 1 is different for each.
- **No equity, no pay.** This draft does **not** give anyone ownership, shares, or payment. Do not promise equity or a share of income in a message or by word of mouth. If that is ever wanted, it needs a company, a solicitor and a separate written agreement.
- **Who is "Cipher" here?** A contract needs a legal person: a company, or an adult acting for the project. People under 18 may be able to cancel contracts, so a parent or guardian should sign too (see the consent block).
- **Student safety comes first.** Cipher is used by students, some under 18. Clause 6 sets the rules for contact and student information.

### 1. Your role

You will help Cipher by acting as: [ADVISOR / CONTRIBUTOR].

| Role | Example description |
|---|---|
| Advisor | Gives guidance on direction, planning and operations. Gives advice only. Cipher makes the decisions. |
| Contributor | Helps with [for example: operations, content, testing, research, design, code]. |

Describe your tasks: [TASKS].

### 2. Unpaid, and not employment

1. This role is **unpaid** and **voluntary**. You are not an employee, partner or agent of Cipher, and you cannot sign anything or make promises on Cipher's behalf.
2. This agreement gives you **no** shares, ownership, profit share or promise of future payment or a job.
3. Anything of that kind needs a separate written agreement, signed by both of us, made after proper legal advice.

### 3. Time

You expect to give about [HOURS] hours a [WEEK / MONTH]. Either of us can change this by agreement. Schoolwork and family come first.

### 4. Who owns the work

1. **Your contributions.** "Contributions" means anything you create for Cipher or while helping Cipher: code, designs, text, images, videos, data, ideas, plans, and improvements.
2. **Cipher owns them.** You assign to Cipher all intellectual property rights in your Contributions from the moment they are created. If the law does not allow that, you give Cipher a permanent, worldwide, free, exclusive licence to use them.
3. **Moral rights.** You agree that Cipher may use, change and publish your Contributions, and you consent to this under Australian moral rights law. Cipher will credit you where reasonable if you ask.
4. **Your own materials.** Anything you made or owned before this agreement, or make separately without using Cipher's confidential information, stays yours. You give Cipher a free licence to use any of it that you include in your Contributions.
5. **Other people's materials.** Do not include anyone else's code, images, music, text or data without permission. For open source code, follow its licence, and tell Cipher what you used.
6. **AI tools.** If you use an AI tool to help create a Contribution, tell Cipher, and do not put Cipher's confidential information or any student's personal information into it.

### 5. Confidentiality

You will keep Cipher's non-public information secret (such as source code, plans, pricing, safety methods and talks with schools or partners) and use it only to help Cipher. This lasts while you help Cipher and for [3] years afterwards. It does not cover information that is public, that you already knew, or that you must share by law. If Cipher asks, you will also sign Cipher's Mutual Confidentiality Agreement.

### 6. Student safety and information

1. **No access to student information** unless Cipher agrees in writing first. Cipher is designed to hold as little student information as possible. Do not collect, copy, save or share any.
2. **Appropriate contact.** Do not contact students privately in a way that could cause harm. Keep any contact with students open, respectful and related to Cipher, and involve a parent, guardian or teacher where appropriate. If a role involves contact with children in person, tell Cipher first so the correct checks can be done.
3. **Follow the safety rules.** Do not change, remove or switch off Cipher's safety features, help numbers, age rules or school-rules guidance without Cipher's written approval.
4. **Report problems quickly.** If you see a safety concern, a data leak, a harmful reply or a complaint, tell Cipher straight away. If someone may be in immediate danger, call Triple Zero (000).

### 7. Honest and careful conduct

1. Do not say or suggest that any school or government body approves or endorses Cipher unless Cipher confirms this in writing.
2. Keep passwords, keys and accounts safe. Do not share them, and use two-step sign-in where it is offered.
3. Follow the law and Cipher's Terms of Use and Privacy Policy.
4. Do not speak to the media or post about Cipher's business plans as Cipher's representative without Cipher's agreement.

### 8. Using your name

Cipher may name you as a contributor or advisor only with your agreement. You may say you help Cipher, but you must not use Cipher's name or logo for anything else.

### 9. Ending this agreement

1. Either of us may end it at any time by telling the other (in writing, including by message or email) with [7] days' notice, or sooner if there is a safety or trust problem.
2. When it ends, return or delete Cipher's materials and accounts you hold.
3. Clauses 4, 5, 6 (as to information already seen) and 10 continue after the end.

### 10. Responsibility

Cipher makes its own decisions and is responsible for them. Advice is general and is not legal, financial or professional advice. Nothing here limits any right that cannot lawfully be limited, including under the Australian Consumer Law.

### 11. General

1. This is the whole agreement about your role. Changes must be in writing and agreed by both of us.
2. If part of it cannot be enforced, the rest still applies.
3. You cannot transfer this agreement. Cipher may transfer it to a company that takes over Cipher.
4. This can be signed electronically and in counterparts.
5. The law of South Australia applies, and we submit to the courts of South Australia.
